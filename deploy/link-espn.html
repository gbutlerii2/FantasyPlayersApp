Link ESPN in one click

ESPN has no sign-in API for fantasy, so nothing can redirect you to ESPN and come back with a token. What does work: the two session cookies your own browser already holds. This page copies them into the app for you.

A bookmarklet does not read your other tabs. It is a bookmark whose address is a small program; it only runs on the page you are looking at when you tap it. So the order matters: be on fantasy.espn.com, then tap the bookmark.

IPHONE — SAFARI, ONE-TIME SETUP

  1. Tap COPY THE BOOKMARKLET below. That puts the program on your clipboard.
  2. In Safari, go to any page and tap the share icon → Add Bookmark. Name it ESPN cookies, save it to Favorites.
  3. Tap the bookmarks icon (open book) → Edit → tap the ESPN cookies bookmark.
  4. Clear the address field and paste what you copied. It must start with javascript:. Tap Done.

IPHONE — EVERY TIME YOU LINK

  1. Open fantasy.espn.com in Safari and confirm you are signed in (your team should show).
  2. Tap the address bar and start typing ESPN cookies — the bookmark appears in the suggestions. Tap it.
  3. You land back in the app with both cookies filled in. Import your leagues.
Safari sometimes will not run a bookmarklet tapped from the bookmarks list — typing its name in the address bar and choosing it always works.
